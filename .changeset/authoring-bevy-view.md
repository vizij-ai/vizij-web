Authoring shows its faces on the Bevy view, over the workspace `@vizij/render` and `@vizij/runtime-react`: the main face, the reference face and the empty-state demo each load as a Vizij, the timeline plays on the face's animation module, the motion-graph editor runs its program as a run, and export writes the scene built headless from the document. The bake steps the face's own composition and stages each track on the key the face plays it on.
